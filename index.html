Eight live instruments built from the problems I’ve actually worked on. Push the robot, tune the equalizer, step through a TSV process, spin a confocal surface scan.

θ0.0°x0.00 mu0.0 N

          
K—

          
          
Cart-pole model of one wheeled leg. The LQR gain is re-solved (discrete Riccati) whenever you change height or weights — taller legs fall slower but need more travel.

        

      


      
      

        

          
Move the pointer to place the wheel. The leg solves its joint angles in real time.

        

          

          

          
hip0°knee0°reach0%

          
Closed-form two-link IK (law of cosines). This is the same math I used for height & pose control on the wheeled biped — set a body height, solve hip & knee.

    03 — Research
    
    
    Making differences measurable.
    
  
    
  
    
    
    
      
    
        
        
    
      
    
      
    Hover to inspect · SURF 2026 poster, with Prof. Peter Bermel
    
    
    
    
    
      
    Non-destructive screening of RP2040 & ESP32 devices
    
      
    Counterfeit ICs threaten reliability and hardware security, and many standards-based checks are qualitative or destructive. I develop measurement procedures and custom fixtures for UV-Vis-NIR diffuse reflectance, and use laser profilometry to compare surface features across suppliers.
    
      
      
        
    • 5suppliers
      • 
        
    • 60specimens
      • 
        
    • 180processed surfaces
      • 
      
    
      
    The practical part: checking that scans capture comparable physical features before interpreting statistics. Sites are kept separate, a site with inconsistent capture is deferred, and supplier-level differences are treated as evidence for further investigation — not a standalone authentication rule.

    MULTI-MATERIAL FDM
    Toolchange simulator
    
      
    
      
      
    layer0tool changes0purges0
    
      
    
      
    Illustrative model of a four-material part. A dual-nozzle machine has to swap filament inside a nozzle (and purge) once more materials than nozzles are in play; a four-head toolchanger parks and picks heads instead. Picking the right machine per job is half of rapid prototyping.
