I’m a computer engineering student at Purdue University. My work connects software with physical systems—from measuring semiconductor surfaces to designing robot controls and pressure-sensing electronics.

I enjoy taking a question into the lab, making a prototype, and learning from what the hardware does. That’s where the interesting problems tend to be.
